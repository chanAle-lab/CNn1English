# CNn1English
A teaching and studying resource for Mandarin speakers to learn English! 为中文母语者设计的英语学习资源。

**Live site:** https://chanale-lab.github.io/CNn1English.github.io-/

## What's inside
- **发音 Sounds** – minimal pairs for sounds Mandarin doesn't have (th, l/r, v/w, long/short vowels…), with tap-to-listen audio (US/UK accent, speed control)
- **词汇 Vocabulary (A1 & A2)** – 492 CEFR A1 words and 862 CEFR A2 words (A–Z) with Chinese meanings, example sentences, audio, search/filters, progress tracking and a word drill
- **短语 Phrases** – flip cards for greetings, daily life, restaurants, travel and work
- **语法 Grammar** – the key differences between Chinese and English grammar
- **纠错 Fixes** – common Chinglish sentences and the natural alternative
- **练习 Practice** – a 10-question quiz with explanations

## Editing content
All lessons live in `assets/data.js`; the vocabulary lists are in `assets/vocab-a1.js` and `assets/vocab-a2.js` (format: word, part of speech, Chinese, example) — add words, phrases, grammar points or quiz questions there. No build step: it's plain HTML/CSS/JS served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch.
